A Letter For You

My Shayla,

I don't know where to begin, because there's too much of you living in my heart to fit into words. From the moment your name first walked into my life, something in me quietly knew you were different — the kind of different that turns into forever.

I love the way you care for people before you care for yourself. I love how your eyes soften when you talk about the things you love, and how safe I feel simply sitting beside you, saying nothing at all. You've seen me at my most tired, my most uncertain, my most ordinary self — and you stayed anyway, choosing me again and again. That kind of love isn't easy to find, and I never want to take it for granted.

Every henna-stained hand we've held, every quiet walk, every silly argument that ended in laughter — I keep them all like little treasures. You are my favorite person to tell good news to, and my safest place when the news isn't good.

So on your birthday, I don't just want to wish you happiness — I want to promise you mine, for as long as you'll let me. Thank you for being my person, my calm, my home.

I love you more than words could ever hold, Shayla. Today, tomorrow, and every year after.

Forever yours,
Nayan
